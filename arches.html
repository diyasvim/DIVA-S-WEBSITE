Arches

From Our Soil to Your Table

Around the lush grounds of Amanzi sits Arches, our restaurant in Pawna Lake, which brings the farm directly to your table. Surrounded by the tranquil beauty of Pawna, our restaurant offers a thoughtfully curated dining experience with generous buffet spreads, warm, attentive service, and flavours guests consistently remember long after they leave. Every ingredient is grown organically at our resort, lending each dish a freshness and finesse that no menu description can quite do justice to. From relaxed breakfasts to indulgent lunches and comforting dinners, dining at Amanzi is effortless, refined, and deeply rooted in hospitality. Pull up a chair, take in the verdant landscape, and let the food do what good food always does: make the moment worth remembering.
